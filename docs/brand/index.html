5 · Rules

Never retype the wordmark

It is a drawing. Typing "SPARK" in a font is a different logo — that is exactly what looked generic before.

One amber per screen

The mark is amber on the icon; in the app header the wordmark is ink, so amber stays for the greeting and the card.

Don't stretch or re-space

Scale from the height. The letters are spaced by hand; spacing them further breaks the lockup.

Clear space

Keep the height of the S free on all four sides of the wordmark, and a quarter of the icon on all sides of the mark.

Minimum sizes

Wordmark 16 dp tall · mark 24 dp · icon 32 dp. Below that the peak's cuts disappear.

Backgrounds

Dark ground always. On amber, the mark goes ink; never place either on a photograph.
